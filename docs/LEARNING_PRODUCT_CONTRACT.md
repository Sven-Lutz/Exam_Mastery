# Learning-product acceptance contract (target; not yet implemented)

**Status:** Requirement baseline. The legacy local engine and outputs have not yet been imported; no item here is a statement of current implementation.

## 1. Learning outcome

Learner can reconstruct core concepts, explain and apply them correctly, derive or prove relevant results, solve novel exam-level questions **without assistance**, and recall and transfer them after delay. Exam grade 1.0 is an aspiration, not a promised outcome.

## 2. Required content

For each relevant competency, adaptive to domain:
- prerequisite knowledge, motivation/intuitive model, precise definition, notation and assumptions;
- complete derivation/proof or explicit explanation why not relevant;
- at least one meaningful worked example and independent counterexample/edge case where relevant;
- original explanatory graphic/animation/interactive representation where it truly adds value;
- appropriate external depth from verified standard texts, original research or authoritative resources, with citations;
- progressively less-scaffolded retrieval, application and transfer exercises, complete independently verified solutions, formative feedback and misconception handling.

## 3. Learning-science functions

Implement evidenced workflows rather than decorative features: active retrieval, spacing, worked-example fading, suitable interleaving, step-based guidance without premature answer disclosure, unassisted exam simulation, delayed assessments and competence-based adaptation. Evidence limitations and domain differences must be documented.

## 4. Mathematics and visuals

Semantically authored equations with defined symbols, readable multi-line proofs, correct subscripts/superscripts, consistent units and rendering across targets. Check edge cases and counterexamples. Use reproducible vector/plot/math animations rather than stock illustration; every diagram must accurately depict a model and have an explanatory caption.

## 5. Scholarly integrity

Trace claims to original course material versus external sources; cite real and relevant sources. External tutorials (including GeeksforGeeks) may support accessibility but do not replace primary scholarship and standard textbooks. Research inserts must add a genuine analytical insight, not name-drop papers. Avoid copyrighted figure reproductions without rights.

## 6. Release mechanism

A release needs successful relevant technical checks, academic spot-checks where applicable and explicit human acceptance of a representative complete learning experience. A critical correctness error forces FAIL regardless of numerical score. Current releases are `HUMAN_REJECTED`; they must not be silently changed to PASS.

## 7. Evaluation and efficiency

Measure correctness, grounded coverage, depth, math/visual presentation, exercise quality, learner UX and delayed *unassisted* performance. Compare to the rejected baseline at equal source inputs, including calls/tokens/cost. No fixed page-count or diagram-count proxies for quality.
