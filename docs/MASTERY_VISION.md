# Mastery — product vision and decision index

## Mission

Mastery transforms supplied learning materials (lecture slides, literature, exercise sheets, solutions, past exams, notes) or an ambitious skill goal into a scientifically informed, personalized learning environment and high-quality materials. The objective is independent understanding, retention, transfer and application at the highest feasible level — not content generation for its own sake.

**University excellence first:** target complete exam-relevant preparation at German grade 1.0 level, with zero known factual errors at release and independent exam practice. No grade or absolute correctness guarantee. Deep understanding is part of the core, not an optional shortcut; extend within the course scope through verified scholarly sources, research and real-world practice. Distinguish confirmed course scope, explanatory depth, optional research/practice and uncertainty. Use expert-curated textbooks as scholarly foundations and standards, not a claim to replace their expertise.

**Long-term:** language learning, durable interconnected knowledge, mathematics, AI/ML, programming/data structures/algorithms/software engineering, research, CAD, engineering, cooking/baking, craft and other skills. Mastery can support an aspiring exceptional developer or chef through theory, practice, feedback and progressively independent tasks. It cannot certify real-world clinical, physical or professional skill from a browser simulation. Different competencies require different learning and verification methods.

## Four integrated learning products

1. **Textbook / course companion**: independently usable course-aligned material, precise definitions, justified derivations, examples, original visuals, research/practice inserts, references and limitations. Curate excellent resources and create explanations as needed; do not copy or pretend generated content supersedes expert texts.
2. **Workbook**: curated and original exercises, complete verified worked solutions, transfer, misconceptions and realistic unassisted exam practice.
3. **Anki**: high-quality precise cards with suitable HTML/CSS, math/media and card types, stable IDs and protected learner edits/history. Anki/FSRS remains the only review scheduler.
4. **Interactive learning**: original 3Blue1Brown-like mathematical storytelling, explorable explanations, interactive derivations, simulations, experiments, coding/tracing/debugging, adaptive assistance and tutoring. Reuse or integrate excellent existing resources where justified; build original resources when useful. Tutor assistance is separate from unassisted assessment.

Obsidian or comparable local knowledge management is an optional linked navigation/personal knowledge layer, not an additional scheduler or competing canonical source. Potential Flourish integration is a future decision, not required for V1. No mandatory extra user-facing product containers.

## Research and quality

Learning science, instructional design, domain pedagogy, high-performance sustainable study, AI tutoring and assessment, learning technology, original visual storytelling, Anki and knowledge workflows are part of the existing R0–R9 programme. Preserve the owner's supplied resources (e.g. 3Blue1Brown, Distill, Seeing Theory, Mathigon, Explorable Explanations, StatQuest, DeepTutor, OATutor, Khanmigo, YouLearn, StudyFetch, Margin Study and other registry entries), verify unclear identities and actual behaviors. Use expert literature, full-text evidence where accessible, null/adverse findings, meaningful comparisons and reproducible evaluation. Do not infer causal learning effects from interface features or model reviews.

An untested design policy is a hypothesis. Technical PASS is not academic release. Record separate evidence for supported practice, independent immediate performance, delayed retention and transfer. Human acceptance remains mandatory; historic HUMAN_REJECTED results cannot be overwritten by CI or an agent. Compare to existing expert resources + direct frontier-model tutoring + Anki at comparable inputs/time, not only to old engine output.

## Constraints

Personal/noncommercial-first, local-first, offline learning, lightweight operation on ordinary hardware (including 8 GB RAM), limited subscription model quotas, explicit authorization for paid APIs, incremental rebuilds, private source and learner data outside the public repo. Do not build a parallel scheduler, broad platform or expensive model committee without evidence and owner decision. Prioritize useful course delivery over more abstractions.
